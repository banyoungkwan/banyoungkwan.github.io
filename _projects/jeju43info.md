## jeju43.info: Building a Digital Ecosystem for Jeju 4·3 Memory

The history of Jeju 4·3 has come a long way — from decades of enforced silence to UNESCO inscription. Yet a critical challenge remains: how do we make this history accessible, alive, and meaningful to a global audience in the digital age? The **jeju43.info** project is my response to that question. It is an effort to build a coherent, open, and sustainable digital ecosystem where the memory of Jeju 4·3 can be encountered, explored, and carried forward by anyone, anywhere.

The project began from a simple observation: despite the wealth of research, documentation, and archival material that now exists, there was no single, integrated digital space that brought it all together in an accessible, public-facing form. Official channels serve important institutional purposes, but they are not designed for open exploration or community participation. This platform aims to fill that gap — not as a replacement for institutional work, but as a complementary space where memory can circulate more freely.

### The Platform

The jeju43.info ecosystem currently consists of four interconnected sites:

---

### 🌐 [jeju43.info](https://jeju43.info)
**The landing page and gateway to the ecosystem.**

This is the entry point — a concise, accessible introduction to Jeju 4·3 for those encountering the history for the first time, as well as a hub that connects visitors to the other platforms. The goal is to lower the barrier to entry, offering orientation before depth.

---

### 📰 [news.jeju43.info](https://news.jeju43.info)
**A living record of ongoing developments.**

Memory is not static. The struggle over how Jeju 4·3 is remembered, taught, and acknowledged continues to unfold in real time — through legislative battles, new excavations, international recognition efforts, and persistent attempts at historical distortion. This platform tracks those developments, creating a running record of the present as it becomes history.

---

### 🗄️ [archive.jeju43.info](https://archive.jeju43.info)
**An open digital archive of historical documents and testimonies.**

Primary sources are the foundation of memory work. This platform provides open access to historical documents, survivor testimonies, official records, and related materials — making the evidentiary basis of Jeju 4·3 history available beyond institutional walls. Accessibility and transparency are its core values.

---

### 📖 [Jeju 4·3 Wiki](https://banyoungkwan.github.io/jeju43-wiki/)
**A collaboratively built knowledge base.**

An ongoing project to construct a structured, encyclopedic knowledge base about Jeju 4·3 — its events, figures, places, and legacy. Built as an open wiki, it invites contribution and reflects the belief that memory is most durable when it is collectively maintained. This project is currently in active development.

---

### Why This Matters

Each of these platforms addresses a different dimension of the same problem: how to keep a history alive and accessible. Together, they form a layered ecosystem — from breaking news to deep archival research, from structured knowledge to open exploration. The underlying conviction is that digital infrastructure is now inseparable from memory infrastructure. To protect a history in the twenty-first century, you must also build the platforms through which it travels.
